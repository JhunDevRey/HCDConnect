## Common problems

**I can't sign in.**
Check the email for typos, and that you're online. If you've forgotten your password, tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen. The [README](README.md#resetting-a-forgotten-password) explains the full steps.

**The password reset email never arrived.**
Check your spam folder, and that you typed the same email you signed up with. For privacy, Firebase doesn't say whether an account exists for an email. If no account uses it, no email is sent.

**I don't see the "New event" button.**
Only organizers and admins can post events. Everyone else is a student, who can view events and RSVP. Ask an admin to make you an organizer of your club. You'll then see the button next time you open the app or pull down to refresh.

**I'm an organizer but can't post for a club.**
Organizers can post only for their own club. The header shows which one, for example "Organizer of Student Council". To change club, ask an admin.

**Event times look wrong.**
The app always shows event times in campus time (Philippine time, Asia/Manila), even if your phone is set to another time zone. This keeps everyone on the same schedule.

**It says "This account has been removed by an admin."**
An admin deleted your account from HCDConnect, so you can't use the app with it. If you think this is a mistake, contact your club or campus admin. Admins can restore accounts.

**I'm not getting event reminders.**
Reminders come an hour before events you've tapped **I'm going** on. Make sure notifications are allowed for HCDConnect in your phone's settings, under **Settings → Apps → HCDConnect → Notifications**. On Android 13 and later, the app asks for permission the first time you RSVP.

## Still stuck?

| What you need | Where to go |
|---|---|
| Report a bug | [Open a bug report](https://github.com/JhunDevRey/HCDConnect/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) |
| Suggest a feature | [Open a feature request](https://github.com/JhunDevRey/HCDConnect/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) |
| Report a security problem | Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Don't open a public issue. |
| Anything else | Contact the maintainer, [@JhunDevRey](https://github.com/JhunDevRey) |

Before opening an issue, search the [existing issues](https://github.com/JhunDevRey/HCDConnect/issues?q=is%3Aissue) in case someone has already asked. Please don't include passwords or other people's personal details in issues or screenshots.

HCDConnect is maintained by volunteers, so replies can take a few days. Everyone taking part is expected to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
